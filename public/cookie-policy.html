What this website stores in your browser, and which outside services it contacts.

1. Cookies set by this website

The public pages of this website do not set cookies of their own, do not use analytics, and do not use advertising or tracking tools. They also do not use localStorage or sessionStorage. Because of that, there is no cookie consent banner. If analytics or similar tools are added later, this policy and the banner will be updated first.

2. Administrator area

The private administrator area (not linked from the public site) keeps a sign-in token in your browser’s sessionStorage while an administrator is signed in. It is removed when you sign out or close the tab. It is not a cookie and is not used for tracking.

4. Controlling storage

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings, and block third-party requests with browser privacy features. The site works without cookies; blocking fonts or images will change how it looks.
