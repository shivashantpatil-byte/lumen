1. Who is responsible

2. What we collect and why

InformationWhy
Name, email address, project type, message (what you type into the inquiry form)To read and answer your inquiry, and to manage it (for example marking it as answered).
Date and time of submission, and a status label added by usTo organise inquiries.
Technical request data (such as IP address and browser type), handled by our hosting provider when you visit or submit the formTo deliver the site, keep it secure, and limit repeated or abusive submissions.

The form has a hidden field used only to catch automated spam. We do not ask for phone numbers, addresses, payment details or any sensitive information. Please do not include such details in your message.

We do not use your details for marketing, profiling or advertising, and we do not sell them.

3. Your consent

By submitting the form you ask us to process the details you entered for the purposes above. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us (see section 8); withdrawing does not affect processing that already took place. If you do not want your details processed, do not submit the form.

4. How inquiries are stored and who processes them

When you press “Send Inquiry”, your browser sends the form data to a server function on our hosting platform. The function checks the data and saves it in a database. You see a confirmation only after the database confirms it was saved. Access to saved inquiries is limited to administrators who sign in with an approved account.

ProviderRole
Netlify, Inc.Website hosting, server functions, request logs.

Google (Google Fonts)Delivers the website’s typefaces. Your browser contacts Google directly, which can see your IP address.

These providers may process data on servers outside India. We rely on their contractual and technical safeguards and will update this policy if providers or regions change. We disclose personal data to others only where required by law or to protect against abuse.

5. How long we keep inquiries

Inquiries are kept for [RETENTION PERIOD, for example 12 months after the last contact] and then deleted, unless a longer period is required by law. Hosting logs are kept according to the provider’s own schedule.

6. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Secret credentials are kept on the server and are never placed in the pages you download. The database blocks public reads, updates and deletes, and only approved administrators can view inquiries. No online system is perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

7. Children

This site is not directed at children. Please do not send us a child’s personal data.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You may ask us to: tell you what personal data we hold about you and a summary of how it is processed; correct inaccurate data; erase your data; withdraw consent; and you may nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you die or become incapacitated. Email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] from the address you used on the form so we can find your inquiry. We aim to respond within [RESPONSE TIME, for example 30 days]. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once that route is available.

9. Applicable law

This policy is written with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind.
