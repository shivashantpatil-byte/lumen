Selected Work

Four rooms, one way of looking at light.

A small, deliberately limited body of residential and commercial work across Mumbai, Alibaug, and Pune — each project developed around the same three questions: how light enters, what the materials will look like in ten years, and what the room is actually for.

These four projects are fictional. Locations, areas, dates and statuses are illustrative, and the photographs are stock images from Unsplash.

Residential

Residence No. 01

Mumbai 2026

A family home reorganized around a single east-facing wall of glass, trading a larger footprint for rooms that only need their lights on after dark.
